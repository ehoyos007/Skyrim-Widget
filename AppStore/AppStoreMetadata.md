## Description (4000 characters max)

Carry the voice of Skyrim with you.

Shouts of Skyrim delivers 300+ memorable NPC quotes from The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim directly to your iPhone — through beautiful, always-on widgets that bring Tamriel to life every time you glance at your screen.

WIDGETS EVERYWHERE
- Home Screen widgets in small, medium, and large sizes
- Lock Screen widgets (circular icon, rectangular quote, inline text)
- StandBy mode support for your nightstand display
- Quotes rotate automatically every 4 hours — a fresh voice of Skyrim throughout your day
- Filter widgets by category: Guard, Companion, Daedric, Dragon, Citizen, Jarl, Merchant, Mage, Thief, or Warrior

SWIPEABLE QUOTE BROWSER
Discover quotes through an immersive card-based browser. Swipe right to favorite a quote, swipe left to move on. Each card shows the full quote, the NPC who said it, their race, location, and hold.

4 HANDCRAFTED DARK THEMES
Every theme is inspired by a legendary Skyrim location:
- Whiterun Warm — muted golds and warm parchment tones
- Winterhold Frost — icy blues and cold silver
- Solstheim Dark — deep reds and volcanic darkness
- Sovngarde Gold — bright golds and ethereal light

Your chosen theme applies everywhere: the app, all widgets, and Lock Screen.

DAILY QUOTE NOTIFICATIONS
Set a daily reminder to receive a featured Skyrim quote at your preferred time. Tap the notification to jump straight to the full quote detail.

QUOTE DATABASE
304 carefully curated quotes spanning:
- 10 categories (Guard, Companion, Daedric, Dragon, Citizen, Jarl, Merchant, Mage, Thief, Warrior)
- 11 races (Nord, Imperial, Breton, Redguard, Dunmer, Altmer, Bosmer, Khajiit, Argonian, Orc, and more)
- 13 holds and regions across Skyrim and Solstheim
- Main quest, faction quests, and DLC content

BUILT FOR ACCESSIBILITY
- Full Dynamic Type support — scales with your preferred text size
- VoiceOver labels on every interactive element
- Reduce Motion support for gentler animations
- High contrast ratios across all themes (WCAG AA compliant)

PRIVACY FIRST
- No accounts, no sign-ups
- No data collection whatsoever
- No third-party SDKs or trackers
- Everything runs 100% on your device
- No internet connection required

This is a fan-made project created with love for the Elder Scrolls community. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Bethesda Softworks, ZeniMax Media, or Microsoft.

## App Review Notes
This is a fan-made quote reference app for The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim. All quotes are sourced from publicly available game dialogue documented on the Unofficial Elder Scrolls Pages (UESP) wiki. The app does not use any copyrighted assets (images, audio, music) from the game — only text quotes attributed to their in-game speakers.

The app collects no user data, makes no network requests, and requires no sign-in. All functionality is entirely on-device.
