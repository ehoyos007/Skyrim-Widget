Privacy Policy

Shouts of Skyrim — Last updated: February 16, 2026

Overview

Shouts of Skyrim ("the App") is designed with your privacy as a core principle. The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Period.

Data Collection

The App collects no data from you. Specifically:

  • No personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • No usage analytics or behavioral tracking
  • No device identifiers or fingerprinting
  • No location data
  • No cookies or web tracking

Data Storage

All app data — your favorites, theme preferences, and notification settings — is stored exclusively on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Network Access

The App makes no network requests. It does not connect to the internet for any reason. All 304 quotes and their metadata are bundled directly within the app. The App works entirely offline.

Third-Party SDKs

The App includes no third-party SDKs, libraries, or frameworks. It is built entirely with Apple's first-party frameworks (SwiftUI, WidgetKit, SwiftData, UserNotifications, AppIntents).

Analytics

The only analytics available to the developer are Apple's standard App Store Connect metrics (download counts, crash reports), which Apple collects independently and which you can opt out of in your device's Settings under Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Notifications

The App offers optional daily quote notifications. These are local notifications scheduled entirely on your device. No push notification server is involved, and no notification tokens are collected or transmitted.

Children's Privacy

The App does not collect any data from anyone, including children. It is rated 4+ and contains no objectionable content.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Since the App collects no data, meaningful changes are unlikely.
